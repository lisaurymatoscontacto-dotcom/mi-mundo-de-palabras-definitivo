## Wikimedia Commons approved pilot photos

The pilot includes three locally optimized photos whose individual file pages, provenance and licenses were checked before approval:

- `Table, 17th century (CH 18493239).jpg` — Unknown artist · Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum — Public Domain.
- `River scene.jpg` — U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service — Public Domain.
- `Door1.jpg` — author Bachrach44 — Public Domain dedication.

These photos are stored locally as optimized WebP files; the published learning experience does not hotlink them. Public-domain files do not require attribution, but Mi Mundo de Palabras retains source, author/provenance and download information for traceability.

## Important distinction

The license of the Wikimedia Commons website text is not automatically the license of an uploaded media file. The importer reads and verifies the **file-specific** license metadata before accepting a photo candidate, and automatic imports remain pending until explicit pedagogical review.
